Auxiliary Verb

am | is | are | was | were | be | been | being

BE

Used to show state, identity, existence, continuous action and in passive voice.

Form Usage Example
am used with I (present) I am happy.
is used with he/she/it (present) She is a student.
are used with we/you/they (present) They are playing.
was used with I/he/she/it (past) He was tired.
were used with we/you/they (past) We were at home.
be base form (used with modals) It will be fine.
been past participle (perfect tenses) I have been there.
being present participle (continuous) She is being honest.

Modal Auxiliary Verbs Part 1 can | could | may | might | will | would

Used to express ability, possibility, permission, willingness and future.

Modal Verb Main Uses Example
ability, possibility, permission I can swim. can
past ability, polite request, possibility Could you help me? could
possibility, permission (formal) He may come. may
less certain possibility It might rain. might
future, willingness, promise I will call you. will
polite request, willingness, conditional Would you like some tea? would

Modal Auxiliary Verbs Part 2

shall | should | must | ought to | need | dare

Used to express suggestions, obligations, necessity, advice and courage.

Modal Verb Main Uses Example
shall suggestion, offer (mainly with I / we) Shall I open the window?
should advice, suggestion, expectation You should study regularly.
must strong obligation, necessity You must be on time.
ought to moral obligation, right thing to do You ought to be honest.
need necessity (present / future) You need not worry.
dare courage, willingness (often in negative) He dared not speak.
