Auxiliary Verb

BE

am | is | are | was | were | be | been | being

Used to show state, identity, existence, continuous action and in passive voice.

Form Usage Example

am used with I (present) I am happy. is used with he/she/it (present) She is a student. are used with we/you/ they (present) They are playing. was used with I/he/she/it (past) | He was tired. were used with we/you/they (past) | We were at home. be base form (used with modals) It will be fine. been past participle (perfect tenses) I have been there. being present participle (continuous) She is being honest.

Auxiliary Verb

HAVE

have | has | had

Used to show possession, experience, completion and in perfect tenses.

Usage

Example

used with I/we/you/they (present)

I have a book.

used with he/she/it (present)

used with all subjects (past)

She has a car. They had finished.

Auxiliary Verb

Modal Auxiliary Verbs

Part 1

can | could | may | might | will | would Used to express ability, possibility, permission, willingness and future.

Main Uses Example

ability, possibility, permission I can swim. past ability, polite request, possibility Could you help me? possibility, permission (formal) He may come. less certain possibility It might rain. future, willingness, promise would polite request, willingness, conditional | Would you like some tea? Modal Auxiliary Verbs

Part 2

shall | should | must | ought to | need | dare

Used to express suggestions, obligations, necessity, advice and courage.

Modal Verb Main Uses Example

shall suggestion, offer (mainly with I / we) Shall I open the window? should advice, suggestion, expectation You should study regularly. ought to moral obligation, right thing to do You ought to be honest. = dare courage, willingness (often in negative) He dared not speak.
